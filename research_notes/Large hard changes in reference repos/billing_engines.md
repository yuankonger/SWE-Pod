## Kill Bill: which projects were the largest and hardest?

### Takeaway
The largest single documented unit of work is the 0.19.x development line that became 0.20.0 (one merge PR of 925 commits, 876 files, +36,093/-22,493), followed by the Hierarchical Accounts project (2015-12 to 2016-08, with draft invoices and parent-summary invoices), the invoice item-tree/repair algorithm and its 0.21.8 behaviour change, and the payment Janitor / two-level payment state machine. Kill Bill does much of its work in long-lived release branches rather than feature PRs, so "multi-PR" evidence is mostly issues, milestones and release notes rather than neat PR series.

**KB-2. Hierarchical Accounts (parent pays for children): draft invoices, parent summary invoices, credit transfer**
- Issue cluster, all opened 2015-12-20 and closed 2016-08-02: #456 "Hierarchical account: account module implementation" (13 comments), #457 "Introduce DRAFT Invoice (required for Hierarchical Account design)" (18 comments), #459 "Invoices changes to support Hierarchical Account" (17 comments); plus #553 "Revisit the credit story for HA parent" (26 comments, the most-commented issue in the repo; 2016-05-25 to 2016-08-02) — [issue search sorted by comments](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=is%3Aissue+sort%3Acomments-desc)
- Design rules settled in #553: the parent builds a daily summary invoice with one `PARENT_SUMMARY` item per child; child credit is not visible at parent level by default; a new API transfers a child's credit to the parent; child `ITEM_ADJ` or repair on an **unpaid** child invoice updates the parent's item, on a **paid** child invoice it is ignored; if the parent invoice is still DRAFT its amount is updated in place, if COMMITTED an `ITEM_ADJ` is added to the parent — [issue 553](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues/553)
- Spanned three repos: killbill #561 (main implementation), killbill #574 (adjustment for unpaid committed parent invoices), killbill-api #14, killbill-client-java #38, plus killbill-integration-tests `test_ha.rb` — [issue 553](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues/553)
- Bugs found mid-project: for child invoices one scenario lost its `CBA_ADJ` +10 item and another added an unexpected `CBA_ADJ` -15 to an earlier invoice (2016-06-09) — [issue 553](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues/553)
- Follow-up double-charge risk: #762 "Unparenting a child whose parent has unpaid invoices can lead to double payments" (2017-06-27 to 2017-08-08) — [issue search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=is%3Aissue+double+payments+unparenting)

**KB-3. Invoice generation as a recompute-and-diff over an item tree; repair items; the 0.21.8 "smallest period" change**
- Algorithm (docs): existing invoices are loaded into one `SubscriptionItemTree` per subscription; previously invoiced items are reversed; proposed items from billing events are matched, repaired or kept; the system "recomputes the full view on each run and diffs it against what is on disk" and generates nothing if nothing changed — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- `REPAIR_ADJ` points to the `RECURRING` item it repairs via `linked_item_id`; earlier invoices are never modified; the repair amount is net of earlier item adjustments (doc example: repair of $239.95, not $249.95, because of a prior $10 item adjustment; resulting `CBA_ADJ` credit $230.32 = $239.95 minus a $9.63 prorated charge for the new plan) — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- Credit rules: `ITEM_ADJ` is negative and linked; if invoice balance is $0 after the adjustment a `CBA_ADJ` account credit is added and consumed by the next invoice; refund-with-adjustment yields only `ITEM_ADJ` and a `REFUND` invoice payment — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- Proration: `IN_ADVANCE` cancellations/plan changes can generate proration credits; `IN_ARREAR` generates none — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- Safety: `org.killbill.invoice.maxDailyNumberOfItemsSafetyBound` (default 15 items per subscription per day); on an illegal invoicing state the account is **parked** and must be fixed then re-triggered via an admin API — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- Issue #1254 "Potential issue with invoice item tree construction" (2020-01-02 to 2020-01-03): overlapping proposed/existing items in BCD updates, mid-period MONTHLY -> ANNUAL upgrades and pause/resume built a wrong tree that still gave correct amounts but risked accounts that "can no longer be invoiced"; fix included a regression test for upgrading 0.20 -> 0.22 — [issue 1254](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues/1254)
- 0.21.8 behaviour change: repairs and re-invoices now cover the smallest possible period. Example: MONTHLY item 1/1/20 to 2/1/20 with BCD change effective 1/15/20 — before: REPAIR 1/15 to 2/1 plus RECURRING 1/15 to 2/15; after: only RECURRING 2/1 to 2/15. Same amount paid, but "upgrading from 0.20.x to 0.21.8 (0.22.x) may generate some additional $0 invoices" — [killbill-0.21.8 release](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/releases/tag/killbill-0.21.8)
- Repair follow-ups: #1221 filter out $0 REPAIR items (2019-09); #1499 prevent voiding a repaired invoice (2021-09, see #1464); #1520 fix an ILLEGAL INVOICING STATE in repair + adjustment (2021-10); #1504 (open) invoice plugins should not be allowed to update `REPAIR_ADJ` items; early data-integrity bug #170 "invoice_items.linked_item_id references non-existent invoice_item" (2014) — [repair search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=repair+invoice+in%3Atitle)

**KB-4. Payment state machine and the Janitor (reconciling UNKNOWN / PENDING with the gateway)**
- Status mapping: plugin PROCESSED -> SUCCESS (201); PENDING -> PENDING (201, e.g. 3-D Secure, ACH); ERROR -> PAYMENT_FAILURE (402); CANCELED -> PLUGIN_FAILURE (502, transaction never happened); UNDEFINED/timeout/exception -> UNKNOWN (503/504) — [Payment userguide](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/userguide_payment.html)
- Reconciliation: explicit (plugin calls `notifyPendingTransactionOfStateChanged` on gateway webhook) or implicit (Janitor polls plugin `getPaymentInfo`). Background Janitor uses a notification queue; on-the-fly Janitor runs on GET with plugin info and before any operation on an existing payment. Defaults: `org.killbill.payment.janitor.unknown.retries=5m,1h,1d,1d,1d,1d,1d`, `org.killbill.payment.janitor.pending.retries=1h,1d` — [Payment userguide](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/userguide_payment.html)
- Retry semantics after Janitor fix: invoice payments made by API call are **not** retried; system-triggered ones **are** retried; the Janitor re-runs the payment control state machine — [Payment userguide](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/userguide_payment.html)
- PR #1216 "Make Janitor notification processing invoke the control state machine": merged 2019-09-19, 9 commits, 14 files, +821/-499; closes #1061 "Janitor UNKNOWN transition may not correctly update invoice balance" (open 2018-10-29 to 2019-09-19) — [GitHub API, PR 1216](https://api.github.com/repos/killbill/killbill/pulls/1216), [janitor search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=janitor+in%3Atitle)
- 21 janitor-titled issues/PRs from 2015 to 2022, including #342 "Janitor lock?", #353, #809 "Janitor updates payment in wrong status" (2017), #1264 "Additional payment events sent during Janitor flow" (2020), #1597 (open PR) "fix race condition in on-the-fly Janitor" — [janitor search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=janitor+in%3Atitle)
- #1599 (open since 2022-02-16): invoice payment runs a top-level attempt/control state machine and a bottom-level transaction state machine; the account lock is not held until the attempt leaves `INIT`, so a plain GET can trigger the on-the-fly Janitor mid-payment; no reliable reproduction; fix proposed in PR #2062 (2024-10) — [issue 1599](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues/1599)
- Related open/closed money bugs: #1755 "Payment state update can lead to wrong invoice balance" (open, 2022); "A pending payment could lead to over-credit" (#1553, fixed in 0.24.0) — [issue search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=is%3Aissue+payment+invoice+balance), [killbill-0.24.0 release](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/releases/tag/killbill-0.24.0)
- 0.24.0 migration replaced the boolean `invoice_payments.success` with a `status` column (default INIT, backfilled SUCCESS where true) — [killbill-0.24.0 release](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/releases/tag/killbill-0.24.0)

**KB-8. Time handling: account reference time, fixed-offset time zone, DST**
- Rule: each account has a `reference_time` (defaults to creation date) and a fixed-offset time zone derived from the account time zone and whether DST was in effect at the reference time (America/Los_Angeles -> -08:00 or -07:00); entitlement/subscription work at time level, invoicing at day-boundary level — [Invoice subsystem doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/invoice_subsystem.html)
- Defects: #139 "Missing invoices due to timezone issue" (2013); #445 "Infinite loop in invoice due to daylight saving" (2015-12); #743 wrong BCD during DST transition (2017); #1934 "Daylight saving issue for usage computation" (2023-11 to 2023-12) and PR #1968 fix timezone issue for usage computation (2024-02, see #1966) — [timezone/DST search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=timezone+OR+DST+OR+daylight+in%3Atitle)

**KB-9. Overdue (dunning) state machine**
- Config: ordered states with conditions `numberOfUnpaidInvoicesEqualsOrExceeds`, `totalUnpaidInvoiceBalanceEqualsOrExceeds`, `timeSinceEarliestUnpaidInvoiceEqualsOrExceeds`, control-tag inclusion/exclusion; per state `blockChanges`, `subscriptionCancellationPolicy` (NONE / IMMEDIATE / END_OF_TERM), `disableEntitlementAndChangesBlocked` (pauses entitlement **and** billing), `autoReevaluationInterval`; emits `OVERDUE_CHANGE` — [Overdue doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/overdue.html)
- Overdue and payment retries are independent systems; retries follow `org.killbill.payment.retry.days` (default `8,8,8`); the doc tells operators to align each retry one day before an overdue boundary; a successful retry returns the account to clear — [Overdue doc](https://docs.killbill.io/latest/overdue.html)
- Bug: #1047 incorrect daysBetweenPaymentRetries in OverdueStateJson (fixed 0.22.0) — [killbill-0.22.0 release](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/releases/tag/killbill-0.22.0)

Notable: #502 "Usage API idempotency" (2016); #1469 usage amount should be floating point not long (2021-08 to 2022-03, shipped with a column type migration in 0.24); #1325 (open) large usage amount could trigger overflow; #1987 (open, 2024-03) "Multiple usage invoice items are created on plan change"; #667 (open) should $0 USAGE items be generated; #1054 (open since 2018) "Finish Usage Price Override feature"; #2152 (open, 2025-09) invoicing externally rated usage — [usage search](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/issues?q=usage+in%3Atitle+is%3Aissue)

### Inferences
- By raw size KB-1 dwarfs everything else, but it is a release train, not one project; the benchmark-usable sub-projects inside it are "add in-arrear billing next to in-advance", "draft and void invoices", "per-tenant catalog price overrides for usage tiers" and "account reference time".
- KB-2, KB-3 and KB-4 are the best-documented *hard* projects: each has an explicit rule table in an issue or doc, follow-up bugs with money consequences, and a behaviour that is observable from outside (invoice items and balances, payment states).
- Kill Bill's persistent bus/notification queue, entitlement-vs-subscription split and multi-tenancy pre-date most of the GitHub PR history searched here; they appear only as side effects (e.g. `STICKY_EVENTS`, "retry handling for failed bus events" in 0.20.0).

### Gaps
- No design docs/RFCs were retrieved: the #1254 analysis lives in an external Google Doc not fetched; the Kill Bill blog and mailing list were not searched.
- AvaTax plugin (killbill-avatax-plugin), invoice plugin API history, multi-tenancy, the entitlement/subscription split, and the bus/notification-queue (killbill-commons) projects were not researched from primary sources in this session; no claims are made about their size.
- Years of 0.20.0, 0.22.0 and 0.24.0 are inferred, not printed on the fetched pages.
- Catalog/alignment doc pages (`userguide_subscription.html`, `plan_alignment.html`) were not fetched; the precise alignment rules are not quoted here.

## Subscriptions on top of a commerce platform that lacks them: what cross-system problems appear?

### Takeaway
Saleor has never had native subscriptions (maintainers said so in 2018 and the community answer was unchanged in 2022); the documented pattern on Saleor, Medusa and Vendure is an external app or plugin plus a scheduled job that creates orders and charges a stored payment method. The best-documented failure modes come from WooCommerce Subscriptions and Shopify: duplicate charges from cloned environments, renewal mode silently flipped by plan switches, storage-migration deadlocks failing 25% of renewals, payment-method rate limits, 3-D Secure challenges on off-session charges, and inventory failures at renewal.

### Cited Findings

**Saleor**
- Issue #2466 "Support for subscription products" (opened 2018-07-24, closed 2018-08-16): maintainer: "there is no built-in support for that in Saleor"; recurring payments deferred to a future payments module. Workarounds posted by users: a cron job creating periodic gateway transactions; a nightly cron creating **draft orders** with staff checking payment manually (July 2020); a proposal to store terms as a product attribute plus a billing module keeping the payment method at the gateway (Sept 2021) — [saleor issue 2466](https://github.com/saleor/saleor/issues/2466)
- Discussion #9359 "Product subscriptions" (2022-03-15, marked unanswered): reply states Saleor does not support subscriptions and suggests building a Saleor app using Stripe and metadata — [saleor discussion 9359](https://github.com/saleor/saleor/discussions/9359)
- Saleor's transactions documentation lists recurring subscription logic as an example use case to build on the transactions API (per search snippet; page not fetched, unconfirmed detail) — [Saleor payments overview](https://docs.saleor.io/developer/payments/overview)

**Medusa**
- Official recipe: custom Subscription module (status active/canceled/expired/failed; interval; period; last/next order date; expiration date) linked to cart, customer and orders. Renewal workflow: get linked cart -> create payment collection -> fetch the customer's most recent saved Stripe payment method -> create and authorize an off-session payment session -> **create a new order by copying the original cart's items, addresses, shipping methods, and tax and adjustment lines** -> link -> capture -> update dates; compensation cancels the new order and restores dates. A daily midnight job processes due subscriptions in pages of 20; failures are only logged — [Medusa subscriptions recipe](https://docs.medusajs.com/resources/recipes/subscriptions/examples/standard)
- The recipe does not cover tax recalculation, inventory reservation or price changes at renewal; it hardcodes the Stripe provider; its cancel route lacks an ownership check — [Medusa subscriptions recipe](https://docs.medusajs.com/resources/recipes/subscriptions/examples/standard)

**Vendure**
- Pinelab Stripe Subscription plugin delegates the schedule to Stripe: subscriptions are created in the background after the customer's setup intent succeeds; only initial payments can be refunded from Vendure, later ones via the Stripe dashboard; cancellation uses cancel-at-period-end; it replaces the instance's single order-item calculation strategy; scheduling was removed from the plugin in its 2.0.0 refactor; a payment eligibility checker exists for mixed carts — [Vendure plugin docs](https://docs.vendure.io/plugins/stripe-subscription), [npm package](https://npmjs.com/package/@pinelab/vendure-plugin-stripe-subscription)

**Shopify subscription contracts**
- Contracts are created by an asynchronous calculate step then a commit; commit can fail with `STALE_CONTRACT` (recalculate) or `NOT_READY_TO_COMMIT`; billing cadence and delivery cadence are separate (prepaid: billing must be a whole multiple of delivery) — [Shopify: build a subscription contract](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/purchase-options/subscriptions/contracts/build-a-subscription-contract)
- Billing attempts take a client-generated `idempotencyKey` "that prevents duplicate payments", are asynchronous (Pending / Success with created order / Failed / ActionRequired for 3-D Secure), and `originTime` shifts fulfilment interval calculation — [Shopify: build a subscription contract](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/purchase-options/subscriptions/contracts/build-a-subscription-contract)
- Limits and failures: do not rebill the same payment method more than 30 times in 35 days or the method is revoked; retry only retryable errors such as `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`; out-of-stock variants fail the attempt with `INSUFFICIENT_INVENTORY` or `INVENTORY_ALLOCATIONS_NOT_FOUND`; success/failure webhooks do not fire until a 3-D Secure challenge completes; Shopify emails the customer when a challenge is required — [Shopify: build a subscription contract](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/purchase-options/subscriptions/contracts/build-a-subscription-contract)

**WooCommerce Subscriptions**
- Model: `WC_Subscription` extends `WC_Order`; dedicated switcher (plan change) and synchroniser (aligned renewal dates) classes — [Woo Subscriptions developer docs](https://woocommerce.com/document/subscriptions/develop/)
- Failed-payment retry: rules are data (interval, customer/admin email template, status to apply to order and to subscription while waiting); intervals accumulate; before each retry the subscription is forced on-hold; retry is cancelled if the order no longer needs payment or statuses no longer match; the retry store itself was migrated from posts to a custom table with a hybrid store and background migrator (from 2.4); docs warn that frequent retries can get the merchant flagged by gateways — [Woo failed payment retry guide](https://woocommerce.com/document/subscriptions/develop/failed-payment-retry/)
- Duplicate charges from cloned sites: staging mode exists because a clone "would also run subscription renewals and charge customers again"; detection compares a stored site URL; a same-domain migration is **not** detected and "your old site will continue to process real recurring payments"; sites reachable at two URLs produce "Payment processing skipped – renewal order created on staging site under staging site lock" — [Woo staging sites doc](https://woocommerce.com/document/subscriptions-handles-staging-sites/)
- Incident, 2026-04-21 to 2026-04-23: subscriptions were incorrectly set to manual renewal and stayed stuck after upgrades/downgrades even with a valid payment method on file; root cause was the switcher not clearing the manual-renewal flag on same-gateway plan changes; fixed in 8.6.1; a Subscriptions Health Check tool to find and fix affected subscriptions was to follow — [WooCommerce developer advisory](https://developer.woocommerce.com/?p=8769973)
- Storage-migration field report (2024-05-01, Universal Yums): about 25% of renewals failed in the first cycle after moving to HPOS due to deadlocks on the order-addresses table (23M+ rows, 120,000+ renewal orders); failure rate by concurrency: 20 batches 25% (12,000/h), 10 batches 10% (10,000/h), 5 batches 5% (7,000/h); workaround re-activated on-hold subscriptions and rescheduled the renewal one minute later. Root cause is the author's hypothesis, not confirmed — [DevPress post](https://devpress.com/woocommerce-hpos-address-table-deadlocks/)
- HPOS data-sync defects in the changelog (start date stored wrongly with sync on; parent-order lookup empty with sync off) come from unofficial changelog mirrors and are **unconfirmed** — [search result summary, e.g. changelog mirror](https://dallas.aiga.org/wp-content/plugins/woocommerce-payments/vendor/woocommerce/subscriptions-core/changelog.txt)

### Inferences
- Mapping to the benchmark's architecture (C# subscriptions app over a Saleor-like API): the problems with direct real-world evidence are (a) duplicate renewal from a second scheduler instance or replayed job (Woo staging; Shopify idempotency key), (b) plan switch corrupting renewal/payment mode (Woo 8.6.1), (c) renewal order built from a stale snapshot of prices/taxes/shipping (Medusa copies the original cart's tax lines), (d) off-session charge needing customer action with webhooks delayed until completion (Shopify), (e) out-of-stock at renewal (Shopify error codes), (f) retry policy as data with order and subscription statuses changing during the wait (Woo), (g) retry-count caps to protect the stored payment method (Shopify 30-in-35-days).
- No primary source was found for Saleor-specific renewal problems (draft-order completion, stock allocation, tax app calls at renewal); these would be designed by analogy, not mirrored from a Saleor thread.

### Gaps
- No evidence gathered on: pausing/skipping, mid-cycle cancellation refunds/credit, price-change notification for existing subscribers, webhook ordering, or stored-card expiry handling for these platforms (Shopify billing-cycle and price-change docs, Woo switching/proration docs not fetched).
- Saleor stored-payment-methods docs URL returned 404; the Saleor version and webhook names for stored payment methods are not confirmed here.
- Licences of Saleor, Medusa, Vendure, the Pinelab plugin and WooCommerce Subscriptions not confirmed in this session.

### Inferences
- For the benchmark's shape (subscriptions app over commerce API with payment app, tax, email, orders, monitoring), the seeds that naturally span the most services are: 1 (multi-entity: numbering, tax, payments, emails, reports), 4 (unknown payment outcome reconciliation between subscriptions app, payment app and orders, with alerts), 6 (tax failure leaves renewal invoice/order unfinalized, retry, no charge until resolved), 7 (dunning grouping overdue renewals into one payment request with emails), 5 (time-zone boundaries with a data migration and a double-billing guard), and 14 (duplicate-scheduler and plan-switch incidents with a repair tool).
- Seeds 2 and 3 are the hardest pure billing-logic problems and have the best published expected outputs, but stay mostly inside the billing service unless combined with order/credit-note effects.
- The ranking is a judgement; hit counts are not comparable one-to-one because Lago title prefixes vary in breadth.

### Gaps
- No lines-of-code totals per Lago series; no release ranges per project; Kill Bill sub-project sizes inside PR #1023 not separable.
- Not researched: Kill Bill AvaTax plugin, multi-tenancy, bus/notification queue, entitlement split; Lago payment-provider integrations and retries, plan overrides, invoice numbering beyond #3394; Shopify billing cycles/price updates; Woo switching/proration and early renewal. These should not be assumed small.
